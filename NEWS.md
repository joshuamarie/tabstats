# tabstats 0.2.0

-   Added `pairwise_matrix()` and `new_pairwise_data()` as generalized
    equivalents of `corr_matrix()` and `new_corr_data()`. The new functions
    work with any pairwise display, not just correlation matrices.
-   `corr_matrix()` and `new_corr_data()` are retained as aliases for
    backward compatibility.
